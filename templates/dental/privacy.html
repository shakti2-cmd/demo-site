1. Form Data & HIPAA Notice

General scheduling forms are designed strictly for callback details (name, phone/email, and preferred window). Do not submit protected health information (PHI) through unencrypted web forms. In this demo preview, form submissions confirm locally without transmitting data.

2. Browser Cache & Storage
