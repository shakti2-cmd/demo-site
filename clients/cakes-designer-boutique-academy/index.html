Meet the Founder

Guadalupe Quaranta

A passionate cake artist, pastry chef, and educator originally from Caracas, Venezuela and now proudly serving Peachtree Corners and Greater Atlanta. With over 22 years of baking experience, Guadalupe founded Cakes Designer Boutique & Academy to craft unforgettable celebration cakes while mentoring aspiring bakers.

Her workshops blend precision technique with warm, bilingual encouragement—helping hobbyists and future bakery owners master structural engineering, flavor balance, and artistic presentation.

My greatest joy is creating beautiful cakes for life’s biggest milestones and teaching others how to turn their passion into a thriving craft and business.
